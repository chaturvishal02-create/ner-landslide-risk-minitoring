# Future Scope & Production Roadmap

1. **In-situ IoT Sensor Grid Integration:** Integrate real-time pore-water pressure sensors, MEMS tiltmeters, and acoustic emission transducers deployed along high-risk highways (e.g., NH-10 Sikkim corridor).
2. **Satellite InSAR & Optical Data Pipeline:** Ingest Sentinel-1 SAR interferometry for sub-centimeter slope displacement detection and ISRO CartoDEM digital elevation models.
3. **Automated Multilingual Broadcast:** Connect SMS, WhatsApp, and CAP (Common Alerting Protocol) APIs to broadcast early warning notices in local dialects (Bengali, Assamese, Khasi, Mizo, Nepali).
4. **Offline Edge Ingestion Nodes:** Deploy quantized Edge ML models on ruggedized Raspberry Pi / Jetson units at remote district checkpoints to maintain warning operations during power grid and optical fiber interruptions.
