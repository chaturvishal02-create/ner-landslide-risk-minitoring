# Project Overview — Landslide Risk Monitoring & Early Warning System

## Smart India Hackathon — Problem Statement 26001

### 1. Executive Summary
Landslides pose a severe annual hazard to life, property, and strategic transit corridors in the North Eastern Region (NER) of India. The primary triggering mechanism is prolonged heavy rainfall acting on steep terrain with vulnerable soil mechanics. This project implements an AI-driven disaster management decision-support system tailored for state disaster management authorities (SDMAs), district emergency operation centers (DEOCs), and local administration.

### 2. Core Objectives
1. **Automated Risk Assessment:** Replace subjective human estimation with an algorithmic, data-driven machine learning scoring engine (0–100 risk scale).
2. **Spatial Situational Awareness:** Present multi-layered GIS mapping for rapid visualization of critical zones across Sikkim, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, Mizoram, Nagaland, Manipur, Assam, and Tripura.
3. **Simulated Stress Testing:** Enable emergency response teams to simulate heavy precipitation surges to model worst-case scenarios and prepare early evacuations.
4. **Actionable Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs):** Accompany risk alerts with concrete mitigation directives (road closures, NDRF mobilization, high-risk settlement evacuation).
